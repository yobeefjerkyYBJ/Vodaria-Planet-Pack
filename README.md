This is an early stage planet pack that replaces the entire stock system with the large binary Higraneus system.

# Planet List

-Higraneus, an average G-type main sequence star only slightly smaller than Sol.

-Rulileah, a 2.1g surface gravity supervodarian world with an atmosphere just thick enough to burn you, but not enough to slow down.

-Vodaria, the birthplace of humanity and the easiest planet to reach in the entire pack.

--Meza, innermost moon of Vodaria, large and tidally locked, it features an extremely thin atmosphere of light scattering dust.

--Pereus, a captured body orbiting Vodaria retrograde, while it's lower gravity should make it an easier landing target, it's reversed orbit will make it a much more solid challenge over Minmus.

-Azuno, red salt flats sit under a cold, dense atmosphere and ammonia rains.

--Ullr is Azuno's lonely moon, rich in potential for refueling due to it's low gravity and surface ice.

-Kalatl, **All these worlds except Kalatl are yours, attempt no landing here.** This planet is due to be changed entirely into a Marslike in the coming months.

--Tena, Kalatl's violently volcanic moon, the source of it's parent world's infamously deadly radiation belts.

-Tyrinnus, the jewel of Higraneus, preferred so much by whatever cruel god gave us Kalatl that it got a ring.

--Terrace, Tyrinnus' crappy little moonlet, barely stable in it's own orbit and frequently bathed in the worst of the gas giant's radiation.

--Denay, the second moon of Tyrinnus, unremarkable save only for it's great views of the giant.

--Vyden, rated the best looking airless body in the system in several entirely fair and untampered polls, this white and gray body (very different to other white and gray bodies) is perfectly positioned to gaze longingly at Tyrinnus.

--Myril, bring a gas mask to this place, it smells _terrible._

-Ososie, the lord of the outer system, a fairly typical L-class brown dwarf of roughly 40 Tyrinnus masses. Before modern science, why exactly the second Sun was so dim and moved like the planets in the sky was a great mystery.

--Thalia, a small lifebearing world in a close orbit to Ososie, it's similar atmospheric composition and gravity to Vodaria make it a prime candidate for colonization.

--Ozaru, unlike the less cool rocky bodies in Tyrinnus' orbit, Ozaru features a thin atmosphere.

--Hikall & Haalin are both wonderful moons, I'm sure, nobody's ever landed on them so we think they may actually be misinformation.

--Ragum is an upstanding citizen moon of Ososie, sitting high in it's inclined orbit, it provides some wonderful views of the system.

-Sozarena, the outermost gas giant in the system, a thin ring surrounding it.

--Aniki is Sozarena's innermost moon, and uniquely covered in dark regolith.

--Leone is a unique moon, notably not because it's actually special in any way, shape, or form, but because...

----Kanaha exists! A moon of a moon, specifically of Aniki.

  Planet surfaces sometimes z-fight

  Kronometer config not entirely precise with the year turnover
